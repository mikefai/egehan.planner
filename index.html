      Study Resources to Reach 1450+

      Curated and prioritized for a 1200 → 1450+ jump in 31 days.

      Official (Free) — Highest Priority

      College Board Bluebook App
      Official · Free · 6+ practice tests
      Same format as the real digital SAT. Take at least 6 full tests. The single most important resource.
      Khan Academy Official SAT Practice
      Official partner · Free · Personalized
      Links to your College Board account. Best for targeted skill building and video lessons.
      College Board Question Bank
      Official · Free · Topic-filtered
      Filter by skill/domain to drill weak areas. Use for error-log follow-up.

      Math (Target: 700+)

      PWN the SAT: Math Guide
      Book · ~$30 · Strategy
      Excellent strategy and tricky problems. Great for jumping 600 → 700+.
      Dr. John Chung's SAT Math
      Book · Advanced · 10 tests
      Hardest math practice available. Use in final 2 weeks for stamina and ceiling.
      Target Test Prep (SAT Math)
      Online · Paid · Structured
      Comprehensive math course with analytics. Good if fundamentals are shaky.

      Reading & Writing (Target: 700+)

      Erica Meltzer — The Critical Reader
      Book · Reading strategy
      Gold standard for SAT reading. Teaches evidence-based reasoning and passage strategy.
      Erica Meltzer — The Ultimate Guide to SAT Grammar
      Book · Grammar rules
      Covers every grammar rule tested. Pair with practice questions.
      1600.io
      Online · Video explanations
      Detailed video walkthroughs of official practice tests. Invaluable for understanding mistakes.

      Question Banks & Drills

      UWorld SAT
      Online · Paid · High quality
      Excellent explanations and analytics. Great for daily drills.
      SAT Black Book
      Book · Strategy
      Teaches how to exploit the test's design. Mindset + strategy focus.
